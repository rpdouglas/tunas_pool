## Find a player

Open **Counter**. Type part of a name in **Find a player**. Each player shows whether they're in this week and whether they've paid.

<Shot name="counter-home-loaded" alt="The Counter screen: Find a player, and the roster with each player's status." />

## Take cash

Take the $20, then tap **Paid cash** for that player. Tapped the wrong one? **Undo cash** puts it back.

## Someone new

**Add a player** and type their name. That's all they need.

## What the counter can't do

Confirm an e-Transfer, change a sheet that's already in, or change anything after picks lock. Those say **Ask the commissioner**. Phone numbers, emails, and picks never show here.
