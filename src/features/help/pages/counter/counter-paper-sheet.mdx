## Enter a sheet

<Steps>
  <li>Find the player on the Counter screen and tap **Enter picks**.</li>
  <li>Go down the sheet, tapping the team they circled for each game.</li>
  <li>Type their tiebreaker: the Monday night total points.</li>
  <li>If they paid cash, tick **Paid cash**.</li>
  <li>**Save picks**.</li>
</Steps>

<Shot name="counter-enter" alt="Entering for a player: the games in sheet order and the tiebreaker." />

<Tip>
  A sheet that's already in can't be changed here. If they want a change, ask the commissioner.
</Tip>
