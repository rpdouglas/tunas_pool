## Your cards

Open **Menu**, then **Tunas 649 Bingo**. Under **My cards**, add a card: pick six numbers from 1 to 49, or tap **Quick pick**. You can have up to five cards. New cards can only be added before a game's first draw.

<Shot name="bingo-cards" alt="My cards: a bingo card with six numbers, none crossed off yet." />

## Each week

Pay for each card by 6 PM Wednesday: cash at the shop, or e-Transfer. After the Wednesday Lotto 6/49 draw, its six main numbers are crossed off every paid card that has them. A week you don't pay for crosses nothing off.

## The board

**Board** shows the pot and how many cards are close. It never shows anyone else's name or numbers.

<Shot name="bingo-board" alt="The board: the pot, and how many cards have 3, 4, or 5 crossed off." />

The full rules and the odds are on the [Rules page](/b/rules).
