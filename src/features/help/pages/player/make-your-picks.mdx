## Pick a winner for every game

On the home screen, tap **Make your picks**. Each game shows the two teams: tap the one you think will win. The bar at the top counts how many you've picked.

<Shot name="entry-form" alt="The picks page: Sunday games, each with two team buttons, and a count of games picked at the top." />

## The tiebreaker

At the bottom of the Monday night game, guess **the total points both teams will score**. If players tie on wins, it decides:

- The closest guess **at or over** the real total wins.
- If nobody is at or over, the closest guess under it wins.

For example, the total is 46. A guess of 58 beats a guess of 45.

## Submit

Under **You and payment**, type the name other players will see (like Dale D.) and say how you'll pay. Then, under **Review and submit**, check your picks and tap **Submit picks**.

<Tip>
  Changed your mind? Open your picks again and tap **Save changes**. You can change them until they lock. After that, nobody can change them, not even you.
</Tip>

Nobody sees your picks until they lock. Then everyone's picks show on the week page.
