## Save an account

Your picks are kept on this phone. To see them on another phone or computer, save an account: open **Menu**, then **Sign in to save your picks**. Continue with Google, or type your email and we'll send you a link.

<Shot name="account" alt="The save-your-picks page, with Continue with Google and an email box." />

The same page has a link to [put the pool on your home screen](/help/home-screen), so it opens with one tap.

## Played before? Link your history

If you played on paper, or on another phone, the pool may already have your weeks. Open **Menu**, then **Link your history**. Type the name the pool knows you by and ask the pool to link it. The commissioner checks it's you, then your weeks and wins show under **Your history**.

<Shot name="claim" alt="The Link your history page. It asks you to save your account first." />

<Tip>
  You need to save your account first. That way your history stays with you, not with one phone.
</Tip>
