## Choose how you'll pay

When you make your picks, **How will you pay?** has two choices:

| Choice | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Cash** | Pay $20 at the shop before picks lock. |
| **e-Transfer** | Send $20 to the e-Transfer email on the page. **Put your name in the message.** |

Then say whether you've paid yet: **Will do** or **Already did**.

You can submit your picks first and choose how to pay later.

## When it counts

The commissioner marks your entry paid when the money arrives. Until then your entry shows **Payment pending**. Only paid entries can win, so pay before picks lock.

The pot is $20 for every paid entry.
