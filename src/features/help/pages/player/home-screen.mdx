You can add the pool to your phone's home screen. It opens with one tap, full screen, with the Tunas badge as its icon. Nothing to download from an app store, and nothing to pay.

## On an iPhone

1. Open the pool in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

## On an Android phone

1. Open the pool in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu at the top right.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**, then **Install**.

## When there's a new version

When the pool has been updated, a strip at the top says **A new version of the pool is ready**. Tap **Refresh** to get it now, or **Later**: it updates by itself the next time you open the pool.

## No signal?

The pool still opens, and a strip says you're offline. Picks you've started are saved on your phone. Send them when you're back online, before the lock.

<Tip>
  Your picks, payments, and other players' details are never kept on your phone, only the pool's
  own pages. That way a shared phone or tablet doesn't keep anything private.
</Tip>
