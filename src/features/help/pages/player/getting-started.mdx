## What the pool is

Each week you pick the winner of every Sunday NFL game, plus the Monday night game. The entry is $20, paid in cash at the shop or by e-Transfer. Whoever gets the most picks right wins the pot.

You don't need an account to play. Open the site and make your picks.

<Shot name="home" alt="The home screen: this week's games, when picks lock, and a Make your picks button." />

## How a week goes

<Steps>
  <li>The week opens. The home screen shows when picks lock.</li>
  <li>Make your picks and a guess at the Monday night total. You can change them until they lock.</li>
  <li>Pay $20, in cash at the shop or by e-Transfer, before picks lock.</li>
  <li>When picks lock, everyone's picks show on the week page.</li>
  <li>After Monday night the winner is posted on the home screen.</li>
</Steps>
