## Thursday to Wednesday 6 PM: payments

Bingo, **Payments**. One tap per card for cash, and **Undo** if you tapped the wrong one. Only the commissioner confirms an e-Transfer, against the bank.

<Shot name="bingo-staff-payments" alt="Bingo payments: each member's cards, with a paid button for each." />

- **Cash handed over before six, typed in after:** use **Received earlier?**, the time, and a reason.
- **After six:** the box at the top asks for one reason, and the money counts for next week.

## Wednesday night: the draw

Bingo, **Draw**. Type the six main numbers from the official Lotto 6/49 result, not the bonus, in one box: `4 5 6 33 34 35`. Then **Save the numbers**.

Someone checks them against the official result and taps **Approve these numbers**. The cards update.

<Shot name="bingo-staff-draw" alt="The Draw screen, asking you to type in the six numbers." />

## A win

The Draw screen says who won and what each gets. A new game opens by itself, and every card carries over. Pay winners from **Payments**, under Winners to pay. The counter pays cash only.
