## Make the week

<Steps>
  <li>Back Office, **Weeks**, **Set up a new week**.</li>
  <li>Pick the Sunday of the week and the week number.</li>
  <li>Get this week's games from the schedule, or paste them: one game per line, in sheet order. The last line is the Monday night game.</li>
  <li>Check **Picks lock** (Toronto time). It defaults to Saturday at 11:59 PM.</li>
  <li>**Save draft**. Players can't see a draft.</li>
</Steps>

<Shot name="admin-weeks" alt="The Weeks screen: each week of the season with its status." />

## Open it

Tap **Preview as a player** to see the sheet the way players will, then **Open week**. Players can make their picks right away.

<Tip>
  The server locks picks at the time you set, whatever anyone's phone clock says. To lock early, use **Confirm early lock**.
</Tip>
