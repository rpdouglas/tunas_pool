## Enter results

Back Office, **Results**. Tap the winner of each game as it finishes, or **Fill in finished games from ESPN** and check them. Then **Save results**. Players see the standings update.

After Monday night, type the **Monday night total points** (both teams together). The week needs every result and that total before it can be final.

## Publish the winner

The screen shows who leads, with the tiebreaker worked out. Check it, then **Publish winner** and **Confirm winner**. The winner shows on everyone's home screen.

When you've paid them, tap **Mark payout sent**.

## A wrong result

**Correct a result**, change it, and say why. Players only see that a result was corrected, and when. If the winner changes, **payout sent** is cleared so you can check it again.
