## Enter a paper sheet

Back Office, **Roster**, find the player, **Enter picks**. The screen follows the sheet top to bottom, so you can read straight down it. Add a photo of the sheet if you have one, then mark how they paid.

<Shot name="admin-enter" alt="Entering a paper sheet: the games in sheet order, the tiebreaker, and how they paid." />

After picks lock, a sheet is a late entry and needs a reason. It goes in the Audit log.

## Import a spreadsheet

Roster, **Import picks from a spreadsheet**. Save your sheet as a CSV file and choose it. Nothing saves until you press **Import**, and each line says what will happen first: **Ready**, **New player**, **Who is this?**, **Already in**, or **Can't import**.

<Shot name="admin-import" alt="The import screen, with a button to choose a spreadsheet CSV file." />

## The roster

The **Roster** lists everyone who plays. Add someone who plays on paper here; they don't need a login. To take someone off, tap them, then **Added by mistake? Delete this player**. It only deletes someone who has never played. For someone who left, untick **Still playing**.
