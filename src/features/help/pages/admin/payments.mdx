## The Payments screen

**Payments** is the first screen in the Back Office. It lists this week's entries, unpaid first. Each shows how the player said they'd pay (**Cash** or **e-Transfer**) and whether they say they already did.

<Shot name="admin-payments" alt="The Payments screen: a list of entries, each with a paid button and a Text about payment link." />

- **Cash in hand:** tap paid for that player.
- **An e-Transfer:** check it arrived in the bank first, then tap paid. Only you can confirm an e-Transfer; the counter can't.
- **Not paid yet:** **Text about payment** opens a ready-made text to that player.

Use **Find a player** to search by part of a name or a phone number.

Every payment you mark goes in the **Audit log** with your name and the time. Only paid entries can win, and the pot is $20 for each.
