## Claims

When a player asks to link their history, it waits under More, **Claims**. A number on **More** says how many are waiting. Check it's really them (the name, the phone number they gave), then approve or reject. Nothing is linked until you approve it.

<Shot name="admin-claims" alt="The Claims screen with no requests waiting." />

## Settling a dispute

More, **Audit log**. Type the player's name or the week in Find. Each line says what changed, who did it, when, and why. For "that's not what I picked", look for *Entered picks* or *Changed the picks*: the before and after hold the picks themselves. A paper entry may have a photo of the sheet.

Nothing in the log can be edited.
